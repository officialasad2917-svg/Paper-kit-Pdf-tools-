Privacy Policy

Your files should stay yours.

This policy explains the intended handling of files and basic information when you use PaperKit PDF Tools.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Files you choose

The current PDF utilities are designed to process selected files locally in your web browser. PaperKit does not intentionally receive the contents of those files on a PaperKit-operated upload server. Browser extensions, your device, and third-party scripts or services may behave independently, so do not use the site for sensitive files unless you are comfortable with your setup.

Third-party libraries and hosting

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages and may load JavaScript libraries from third-party content delivery networks to perform PDF and image operations. Those providers may receive routine technical information when your browser requests their resources, according to their own policies.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This site may not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools at present. If these services are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them and any choices available to visitors. Your browser or hosting provider may still process technical data as part of delivering the website.

Children

PaperKit is a general-purpose utility website and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The date above indicates the most recent revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project through the GitHub issue page. Do not post private or sensitive information in a public issue.

Note: This is a starter privacy notice, not legal advice. Review it and adjust it to match the website’s actual behavior before relying on it for legal compliance.
