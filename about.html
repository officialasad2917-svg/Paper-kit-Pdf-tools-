About Us

Learn about PaperKit, a collection of simple browser-based PDF utilities.

What is PaperKit?

PaperKit is a lightweight collection of online tools designed to help with common PDF and image tasks, including merging PDFs, splitting pages, rotating PDFs, converting images to PDF, and exporting PDF pages as PNG images.

Designed for simple tasks

Our tools are intended for everyday document tasks. Processing takes place in your browser using JavaScript libraries loaded from public content delivery networks. An internet connection is needed to load these libraries.

Important note

Always review downloaded files before sharing them or relying on them for important work. Very large documents may be slow or fail on devices with limited memory.
