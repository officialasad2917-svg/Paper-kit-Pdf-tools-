How to use PaperKit

  1. Choose your file(s).
  2. Tap the matching tool button.
  3. Wait for processing, then save the download.

Frequently asked questions

Are the tools free?
Yes, the tools on this page are free.

Are my documents uploaded?
No. Selected files are processed locally in your browser.

Why did a large file fail?
Phones have limited memory. Try smaller files or fewer pages.
