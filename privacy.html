Privacy Policy

Read how PaperKit handles files and basic website usage.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Files you select

PaperKit's PDF tools process selected documents locally in your browser. The website code is designed not to upload those selected files to a PaperKit processing server. Do not use the tools for highly sensitive documents unless you are comfortable with your browser, device, and loaded code.

Third-party content delivery networks

The site loads PDF and ZIP libraries from public CDNs. Your browser connects to those providers to download the libraries. Those providers may process connection information under their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information or contact method published on the Contact page.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the website changes. Check this page for the current version.
